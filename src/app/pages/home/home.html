Poke Finder is a small project - half Pokedex, half feedback box. It pulls real data from PokeAPI, so the Pokedex page pages through all 1300+ Pokemon instead of faking a handful.

Open the Pokedex Leave feedback

Why this exists

I wanted a small, honest example of an Angular app that actually talks to something outside itself - no mock JSON, no lorem ipsum. The Pokedex page hits a live public API with real pagination, and the feedback form is a real reactive form with validation, not just a wall of inputs.

What's under the hood

  • Angular Router for the three pages you can see in the nav bar
  • A PokeCatalog service wrapping HttpClient
  • A reactive form on the Feedback page with live validation messages
